PDZ adaptive faction masters (v0.3)
-----------------------------------
The files named theme_*.ogg are alternate masters derived from the credited
v0.2 source tracks below. They add transition-safe fades and faction-specific
spectral mastering; the original track authors and licences remain unchanged.

PDZ combat revision v0.4
------------------------
combat_heavy_battle.ogg repairs two beat-sized catches in the CC0 Heavy Battle 2
source with short equal-power edits.
combat_orchestral_assault.ogg is a slower, longer rearrangement of the CC0 Hope
and Determined Pursuit sources with a quiet Dirty Rain ambience layer. All
source creators and links are listed above.

PDZ location and boss suites v0.6
---------------------------------
The files theme_*_dawn_patrol / road_embers / last_watch / scrap_horizon /
faded_orders / white_room / ghost_signal / quiet_hunger, the two
combat_infected_* cues, and boss_01_* through boss_13_* are alternate masters
and long-form suites derived exclusively from the CC0 sources already listed
above. Passages were reordered and crossfaded, then given encounter-specific
tempo, spectral, ambience and loudness treatment. No additional third-party
music or new licence was introduced.
